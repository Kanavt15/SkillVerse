# Code of Conduct

SkillVerse is about people helping people learn. We expect the same spirit from everyone who contributes to it.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Share knowledge. A good explanation in a review is worth more than "fix this".

Unacceptable: harassment, discriminatory language or jokes, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the project maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments or contributions, or ban contributors, who violate this code.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
